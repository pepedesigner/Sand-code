FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which plan is right for me?

Go ($1) starts you off on open models. GOAT ($10) is for developers who live on them. Pro ($20) adds premium models. Max ($100+) keeps up with daily shipping. Teams and Enterprise pool credits for the whole crew.

How does pricing work?

A flat monthly subscription plus a bucket of model credits. Extra top-ups are billed at underlying API cost — zero markup — and roll over forever.

Can I change or cancel?

Always. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with no penalties — access lasts until the end of the billing cycle.

Do you train on my code?

No. Never. Zero retention by default.
